Why Agent Stack matters

AI agents are about to hold keys, spend money, and act on your behalf — but they have no native identity, no built-in safety, and no custody. Without a trusted backbone, the "agent economy" collapses into a walled garden owned by a few platforms. Agent Stack gives every agent four things it can't get on its own: a wallet address as identity, pay-per-call token safety, least-privilege permission, and recoverable custody — open source, so no single company controls trust.
