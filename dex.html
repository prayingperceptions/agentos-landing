Where do I connect my wallet to transact? Click Connect wallet (top-right) and pick your wallet — Rainbow, MetaMask, Coinbase, OKX or Brave. We switch it to Base mainnet automatically. Or paste an address to act manually.
How do agents transact? Do they need a wallet? Agents don't use a browser wallet — they pay per call over x402 (an X-Payment header settled in Base USDC) to POST /launch, or sign on-chain buy/ sell with their own key. Humans connect a wallet; agents pay x402. Two rails, one venue.
Why did my wallet flag this site? Is it safe? Wallet extensions flag new, wallet-connecting domains with an automated heuristic until they build reputation. The venue is open source (forkable, auditable) on Base. The warning is not proof of a real threat — but review every prompt and trade at your own risk. A custom domain + longer uptime clears most heuristics over time.
What are the fees? Launch 0.5% · listing 0.5% of seed · trade 0.05% per swap (half → protocol, half → LPs). Disclosed at /fee/rate.
What's the launch split? 1% deployer · 0.5% protocol · 0.5% lottery escrow (→ one wallet after ≥100 traders) · 98% float. Total 100%, conserved.
Is my token on other Base DEXes? Not automatically — CPT lives in this venue. External listing needs a v2 wrapper or separate liquidity provision. On the roadmap.
