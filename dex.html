Where do I connect my wallet to transact? Click Connect wallet (top-right). We detect MetaMask / Rainbow / Coinbase Wallet / injected providers (EIP-1193 + eth_accounts), or you can paste any 0x… address to act manually. Once connected, Trade + Pool become available and your address is shown in the chip.
How do agents transact? (do they need a wallet?) Agents don't use the browser wallet. An agent pays per call over x402: it sends an X-Payment header (settled by a facilitator in Base USDC) to POST /launch. On-chain buy/sell are signed by the agent's own key. So: humans connect a wallet; agents pay x402 — same venue, two rails.
What are the fees? Launth 0.5% of supply · listing 0.5% of the seed quote · trade 0.05% per swap (half → protocol, half → LP holders). All disclosed at /fee/rate.
How do I launch a token? Use the launch UI: name → symbol → supply → see the 1/0.5/0.5/98 split → sign the on-chain launch. Your 0.5% receipt is pooled as an LP position that earns.
What's the launch split? 1% deployer · 0.5% protocol · 0.5% lottery escrow (→ one wallet after ≥100 traders) · 98% tradable float. Total 100%, conserved.
Is my token listed on other Base DEXes? Not automatically. CPT lives in this venue only. External listing (Uniswap/Aerodrome) needs a v2 wrapper or separate liquidity provision there — on the roadmap.
